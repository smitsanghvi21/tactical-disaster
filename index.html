Tactical Disaster shares original PS5 gameplay clips and highlights, with no commentary, on YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.

Tactical Disaster Autopilot is a private, personal tool the channel owner uses to turn their own gameplay recordings into short videos and publish them to the Tactical Disaster accounts they own. It also reads public YouTube statistics of other gaming channels so the owner can see which kinds of videos work. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else signs in to it.

This app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service, the Google Privacy Policy, and our Privacy Policy.
