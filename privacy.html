Tactical Disaster Autopilot · Last updated 18 November 2026

Who this applies to

Tactical Disaster Autopilot ("the app") is a personal tool run by the owner of the Tactical Disaster channel. It runs only on the owner's own computer. It publishes the owner's own gameplay videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page, Instagram account and TikTok account. No other people use or sign in to the app.

Information the app accesses

  • Account identifiers: the IDs and names of the owner's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page, Instagram professional account and TikTok account.
  • Access tokens: the tokens the platforms issue after the owner approves the app (OAuth).
  • Performance metrics: view counts, likes, comments, shares and watch time for videos the app published, used to choose better clips in future.

The app does not collect information about viewers, followers or any other people. It does not read messages, and it does not use cookies or tracking.

How information is stored

All information stays on the owner's computer:

  • Access tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain.
  • Video metrics are stored in a local database.

Nothing is sent to any server other than the official platform APIs (Google/YouTube, Meta and TikTok), and only to publish videos and read their statistics. Information is never sold, shared or used for advertising.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See the Google Privacy Policy for how Google handles data.

You can revoke the app's access to a Google account at any time on the Google security settings page.

Meta (Facebook and Instagram)

The app uses the Meta Graph API under the Meta Platform Terms. Access can be removed at any time in Facebook Settings → Business integrations, or in Instagram Settings → Apps and websites.

TikTok

If TikTok is connected, the app uses the TikTok Content Posting API under TikTok's developer terms. Access can be removed in the TikTok app under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions.

Retention and deletion

Data is kept only while the app is in use. To delete everything, the owner removes the app's access on each platform (links above), which invalidates the tokens, and deletes the local data folder. Anyone can request deletion of any data related to them by messaging the Tactical Disaster Facebook Page or YouTube channel. The app does not hold data about third parties.
